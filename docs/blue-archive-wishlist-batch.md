# Blue Archive wishlist batch — 2026-09-10

76 additional game-asset scenes were restored, rendered, visually sampled and imported through Idlesse’s native Library panel. Together with seven previously imported wishlist entries, this covers 83 of the 112 requests. The other 29 entries were excluded because they depict underage characters sexually; no derivatives of those scenes were produced in this batch.

All new clips are 3840×2160, 60 fps, HEVC. This is restored/upscaled texture detail, not native 4K source detail. Spine-only rendering may omit Unity effects or physics. Complete authored idle cycles are preserved, including Akari’s separate 66.67-second background and Seia’s 77.67-second loop.

Every clip passed full decoded-frame verification. First/middle/last frames were inspected; six framing corrections were re-exported and verified before import. These samples do not establish exact in-game visual parity. The active Kayoko Dress wallpaper was preserved.

The videos total 3.48 GB. Originals/restored textures, provenance and compact posters were copied into the existing Idlesse Drive hierarchy with SHA-256 verification. Remote Drive upload remained unconfirmed at completion; playback files and extracted originals were retained locally.

Six bounded Modal restoration jobs completed. Export now uses WebCodecs HEVC directly from the canvas, a shared encoder lock, progress watchdog, resumable checkpoints and full hardware decode verification. A 200-frame Yuuka probe encoded in 5.06 seconds (8.12 seconds for the whole invocation); long or complex scenes remain slower. No claim of a universal speedup or a recurring cloud service.

See [workflow](../scripts/media-batch/README.md) and checked-in plans for reproduction. Game assets and personal Drive identifiers are not included in Git.
